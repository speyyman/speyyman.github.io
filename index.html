#1 Who

Pl. I

Hi, I’m Saeed.

I build software and data systems: the backends, pipelines and tools that other things depend on. A lot of my work starts with a system that has become hard to change. I read it until I understand what it really does, then rebuild the parts that need it, in small steps that are easy to check.

I also make procedural images, short films and long write-ups about patterns in nature, like the drawings on this site. The two kinds of work help each other. Both reward patience, and both come down to finding the simple rule under something complicated.
