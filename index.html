#1 Who

Pl. I

Logic with a pulse.the pulse is the messy half

Pattern first · Then the plan

I like problems that don’t fit on one page: systems with too many moving parts, data that refuses to line up, ideas that need a shape before they need a plan. The work is finding the one thread that, pulled gently, loosens the whole knot.

The other half of me makes things because they should exist: images, short films, long write-ups on how patterns form, this site. I don’t think the halves are separate. Good engineering has taste, and good art has structure.
