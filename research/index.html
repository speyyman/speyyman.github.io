Research

Forecasts, models and careful questions about messy data. I care as much about where a result breaks as where it holds.

How I work

I clean before I model, and I record every value I had to fill in, so I can say how much of a result rests on guesswork. I set a simple baseline first, so every model has to beat something honest.

I score models on stretches of time they have never seen. It is a stricter test than most, and it is the only one I trust.

Questions I keep returning to

How much of a forecast is skill and how much is luck? When two sensors disagree, which one do you believe? What does a model look like when it is honest about not knowing?
