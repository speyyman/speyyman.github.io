Visual essay

Engravings, drawn live

A fern, a sunflower and the moon, each drawn from its rules, one step at a time. One minute and forty-six seconds, silent, with captions.

What you are watching

Three plates, each drawn by a program from a few rules, shown while the rule runs.

The fern is Barnsley’s: four affine maps and a loaded die. The film plots the first points one at a time, with the chosen map lit in the readout, then speeds up until 334,176 points have landed and the fern is there.

The sunflower is Vogel’s model: each seed turns 137.508 degrees from the last and steps out a little. The first seeds go down one by one, then the rest, 619 in all. Then the angle changes to 135 degrees, which makes eight straight rows, and to 137, which tears the head into arms, before going back.

The moon is engraved row by row under full light, then the light turns to where it was at midnight UTC on 23 October 2026: about 139 degrees from the sun, 88% lit, waxing gibbous. The dark side is drawn faint so the face stays legible. The seas are placed by hand and the craters at random. The light is the only part that follows a rule, and the film says so.

How it was made

Every frame is drawn on a canvas in a headless browser by the same code that draws the plates on this site, plate-engine, and dithered into the site’s three tones. A script renders the frames one at a time, at 30 per second, and hands them to FFmpeg, which encodes this version, a WebM for the page, and a vertical cut. Nothing is filmed and nothing is drawn by hand.

The render is deterministic. The script records a SHA-256 hash of every frame. Its end-to-end test renders two stretches from the middle of the film twice and checks every frame comes out byte for byte the same, and, when a full render is present, that the stretches match it frame for frame. The finished fern and sunflower are tested cell for cell against plate-engine’s own plates.

The film is silent. The captions are part of the picture and are also in the captions track, from the same script.
