About

I untangle complicated systems and make things that feel good to use. This is the short version of who I am and what I’ve done.

In short

I’m a software and data engineer who now works independently. I studied software engineering in Kabul, started shipping telecom backends while I was still a student, and later moved into data engineering and a master’s in data science in Michigan. Today I work from Nashville.

I’m drawn to problems with too many moving parts: systems that grew without a plan, pipelines that carry something important, data that refuses to line up. My habit is to look for the pattern underneath. Once I can see the one thread that holds the mess together, the rest is careful, unglamorous work.

Outside client work I make images, short films and long write-ups, and I run a few experiments of my own, including this site. I don’t think the two halves are separate. Good engineering has taste, and good art has structure.
