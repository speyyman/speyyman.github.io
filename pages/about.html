About me

Engineer by trade, data scientist by training.

I’m Saeed Peyman, a software and data engineer with 7+ years of experience building APIs, data pipelines and cloud systems, from telecom networks in Kabul to healthcare platforms in the US.

My story

Hello! I build systems that move data.

I started my career in 2019 as a full-stack engineer at KeyBytes in Kabul, building APIs and messaging infrastructure for a telecom provider while finishing my BSc in Information Technology at the American University of Afghanistan.

From there I joined the MIT ReACT certificate program for displaced students and professionals, then earned an M.S. in Data Science at Michigan Technological University on a full scholarship from the Institute of International Education. Along the way I taught a graduate data science lab and worked as a data engineer on an AWS migration and a HIPAA-compliant API.

Most recently I worked as an independent contractor, architecting a streaming platform built for low-bandwidth listeners. I’m now looking for full-time data and engineering roles as well as freelance projects. I care about systems that are reliable, well documented and designed around the people who depend on them.
